What you can judge now.

The six scenes keep the accepted composition and short copy. This pass adds movement between the objects and sections:

  • The hero token responds to pointer movement, press and release with elastic deformation. WebGL deforms the existing artwork, with a Canvas fallback; it is not a finished 3D model.
  • A shared paper travels from Ada’s envelope into the activity report. One visible paper travels into place while scrolling stays under your control.
  • Each floor has its own office details. Lift doors, lights, an arrival indicator and a small report movement form a finite sequence. A desk cup responds to the envelope handoff.
  • The origin illustration changes its SVG outline from an office window to a phone as you scroll.
  • The closing token reuses the same finite material response and settles once beside the wordmark.

The playable activity, Ada’s current reactions and floor controls remain in place. The office and floor illustrations are website art, not production app screenshots. The device’s reduced-motion setting keeps the settled poses.

Finished details.

Ada keeps one textured character master with clean silhouette masks and independent acting. The report receives an ink stamp or date cues, and the closing token uses the same fluid material as the opening. The six scenes share one composition and motion language.

Ten authored sequences and five interaction transitions form the website. They are responsive browser scenes, not separate video files. Browser and responsive checks are recorded separately from physical-phone measurements.
