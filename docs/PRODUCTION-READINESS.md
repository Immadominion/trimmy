# Production readiness

Status checked September 28, 2026. Trimmy has a working game, a guarded Solana trading path, sends out of the wallet and wallet recovery. Legal review and a few operator settings remain before broad real-money use. This audit separates technical checks from completed transactions and release requirements.

## Verified and fixed

- **Every listed stock and fund token, found automatically:** the token directory admits each token the Market's curated source lists whose mint carries a supported issuer's pinned identity; no token is approved by hand and no deploy is needed. On September 28 that was 541 tokens (119 xStocks, 40 Backpack, 371 Ondo, 8 PreStocks and 3 Tessera), including the S&P 500, Nasdaq, gold, silver, oil and copper funds; it refused 8 (one Ondo token whose freeze authority differs from Ondo's, and 7 gold tokens from non-stock issuers). The 84 tokens proven earlier seed the directory.
- **Order verification:** every order is reviewed and simulated before signing, and priced against trusted prices (`stock-token-prices.ts`): the issuer's own share price from Jupiter's `stockData`, and the token's traded price only when it has at least $10,000 of liquidity. More than 3% worse than every trusted price (plus the token's own fees) is refused as off market; with no trusted price, Jupiter's price impact must stay under 5%. Before September 28 the check compared orders with the token's last traded price, which for thin tokens can be absurd (PYPLx traded at $248,954 on $0.05 of liquidity while the share was $54), so a buy far above the share price could pass; prices are converted to raw token units with the token's current share multiplier. If a token's usual route has no quote, the other route (aggregator or market maker) is tried; Ondo's tokens trade only with market makers. Sells are no longer capped per token, only by holdings and this price check. Earlier, 84 tokens each passed an unsigned buy and sell review and simulation before admission.
- **Issuer terms and an honest Market:** preview requires the current eligibility statement for the token's issuer (409 `TERMS_REQUIRED`) and stores the acceptance with the order. New builds read capabilities v2: each issuer's disclosure, warning and offer status, and a reason for every Market token that cannot be traded. Installed builds keep the original xStocks-only capabilities and checkbox.
- **All five issuers offered, each with its own warning first:** PreStocks and Tessera are offered at the owner's direction; their disclosures state that PreStocks names no legal issuer, that Anthropic and OpenAI say such share transfers are void, that PreStocks charges a 3% fee on every transfer, and that a Tessera claim is lost if its redemption window is missed. On September 28 read-only $2 buy and sell quotes for AAPLx (xStocks), SKHY (Backpack), AAPLon (Ondo, regular session), ANTHROPIC (PreStocks) and tOpenAI (Tessera) all passed the price check; nothing was signed.
- **Market-maker (RFQ) orders:** Ondo's liquidity exists only with JupiterZ market makers. The review accepts exactly one market-maker co-signer as fee payer, decodes the order engine's `fill` instruction, requires the maker's own accounts and the exact quoted amounts, and simulates; settlement finds the maker-signed transaction through the user's signature. Ondo's makers mint just in time, after the user signs, so a buy's simulation stops at the maker's transfer. The review accepts that one failure only when the logs show the user's transfer completed and only the maker's delivery failed for insufficient funds, with nothing after it; the fill is all or nothing, and the reviewed terms say so. Twelve Ondo tokens that trade 24/7 passed real buy and sell reviews in Ondo's weekend off-hours window on September 27, the first end-to-end proof of this path. Installed older apps never receive an RFQ order.
- **Honest refusals:** a token that cannot be traded says why: its issuer could not be confirmed, its rules restrict transfers, or its issuer is not offered. Market hours, missing market makers and off-market prices are answered per order, with the token's market state shown before anyone orders.
- **Market states:** capabilities v2 gives every token its state now: around the clock, or Ondo's own sessions per token (overnight, pre-market, regular, after-hours, and weekends for some). Ondo's live status page decides, with the published session calendar (pauses, NYSE holidays, early closes, daylight saving) as the fallback, and any mint paused on chain reads as paused by the issuer. Closed tokens cannot be ordered and say when they open; Ondo tokens refused only because their market was closed say when it opens, and read as awaiting review while it is open. A market-maker refusal of an order under the $1 minimum is reported as that, not as a closed market.
- **Fee and share guards:** a buy is refused if a token's transfer fee rises above its admitted rate. Orders in a fee-bearing token widen the slippage tolerance by that fee, because Jupiter quotes before it; the review checks the simulated net delivery. Reviewed terms carry the stock's scaled display multiplier so the app shows shares, not raw units. The review's account read now allows up to 4 MiB: routes through one market maker carry a ~720 KB venue account and were refused every time before. Routes through a third token may use the user's own account for it, and routes may close the user's existing wrapped-SOL account back to SOL; every other token account of the user's in a transaction must end with exactly its starting balance in simulation.
- **Decoder fix with user impact:** SPL Token leaves the old bytes behind an empty option tag after a revoke, which is valid state. The strict account decoder rejected such accounts, so any user whose USDC account ever had a delegate revoked would have had every order refused. Those unused bytes are now cleared before the strict comparison; any other malformed layout still fails.
- **Balances and recovery:** holdings V2 aggregates validated stock accounts and distinguishes total holdings from canonical-account spendable balances. Token-2022 display scaling is preserved. The mobile flow uses trading capabilities, refreshes wallet state, and reconciles pending orders before another submission. Older holdings clients retain V1 responses.
- **Immediate sell readiness:** there is no holding cooldown. A pre-trade request could supply stale balances after confirmation, a zero sell amount could remain frozen, and transaction review checked finalized balances while the app showed confirmed holdings. The fixes force a new post-mutation read at the confirmed slot, reactively populate untouched sell amounts, refresh real routes on return, and use confirmed wallet balances and simulation with a minimum slot. Mint and program identities remain finalized. Tests cover a newly funded account absent from the older finalized snapshot, frozen accounts, paused mints and stale RPC responses. Missing or malformed settlement outcomes stay unresolved; simulation requires explicit success.
- **Server deployment:** since September 28 the Railway `api` service deploys from GitHub `main` on every push that touches the API's build inputs (other paths are skipped); laptop uploads are no longer the deploy path. `d12bd00` is live in deployment `21f610e8`, with readiness 200 and trading enabled. The `/ready` health check keeps a broken build from replacing a working one. Capabilities schema 3 lists 542 tradeable tokens. Unauthenticated previews, sends and history return 401. Browser access is limited to the configured origins, including the web app at `app.trimmy.xyz`, which is deployed to Vercel from the same `main`.
- **Holdings value and total balance:** `/v1/markets/stocks/prices` gives a trusted price per displayed share for held tokens: a deep market's price (at least $100,000 of liquidity), else the issuer's, else a liquid market's. The phone and web multiply it by the wallet's display amount; a token with no trusted price shows no value and the card shows cash alone. On September 28, 523 of 542 tradeable tokens had a trusted price. The total is USDC counted at $1 plus stocks; SOL is shown separately as the fee reserve.
- **Sending out:** USDC, SOL and any Trimmy stock token (including ones no longer traded) can be sent to another Solana wallet from the phone and the web. The server builds the transfer, refuses destinations that are token accounts, mints or programs, sends to oneself, paused or hooked tokens and SOL amounts that would strand a wallet, simulates it and returns it unsigned with a 90-second review token; it sends only the exact reviewed message signed by the account's own wallet. Mainnet simulations of USDC, SOL and AAPLx sends (to a new wallet, creating its account) passed read-only on September 28; no send has been signed yet.
- **Wallet recovery:** the recovery page is served by the web app at `app.trimmy.xyz/wallet-recovery` with the web Privy client, and the phone's Settings shows Back up wallet again. Signing in there and matching the wallet still needs the owner's walkthrough.
- **Order fills:** a confirmed order's actual input and output are read from its transaction and recorded once (migration `0033`); history shows "You paid" and "You received" beside the reviewed quote. Migration `0033` is written and tested but not yet applied to production; until it is, history shows quotes as before.
- **Operator alerts:** with `TRIMMY_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` set to a Slack or Discord webhook, the API posts on 500 errors, failed or never-executed signed orders, two failed token sweeps in a row and unhandled errors, rate-limited and without user, wallet or amount data. The variable is not set yet.
- **Backups:** a Railway cron service dumps the database daily at 03:00 UTC to an S3-compatible bucket (11 dumps on September 28). The September 28 dump was restored into a disposable PostgreSQL 18 cluster with no errors (73 tables, 643 rows, functions and triggers intact), then deleted.
- **Seeker device build:** build **2026092806** (APK SHA-256 `0902b4f7…`, development certificate `95ba33d5…`) was built with the stale native libraries removed, checked for current compiled Dart, and installed with `adb install -r` on September 28; app data was preserved. A read-only walkthrough showed a $5.02 total balance ($3.00 cash and $2.02 of AAPLx at its trusted price), Fast buy, Add money and Send on one line, the Send sheet listing 3 USDC ready to send, and Back up wallet in Settings. Nothing was quoted, signed or sent.
- **Submitted-order history:** authenticated, paginated Trimmy orders now have pending/confirmed/failed/expired states, explorer links and automatic pending reconciliation. Account changes clear prior rows; pagination and return-from-stock refresh have regression coverage. Amounts are reviewed quotes until a fill is recorded (see Order fills). Migration `0032` and the history API are deployed. Production readiness, unauthenticated rejection (401), and allowed-origin history/status preflights (204) passed; authenticated history still needs an on-device walkthrough.
- **Gameplay and feedback:** Career/community browser preflights, stale draft revisions, lost draft-save responses and failed sound preparation now have regression coverage. Paper and Real ledgers remain separate.
- **Reminders:** native Android/iOS local scheduling and Career tap routing are implemented for daily or Monday/Wednesday/Friday check-ins around 7 PM local time. This is not a social or transaction push-delivery system.
- **Checks:** on September 28 the backend suite passed **849 tests**, the full mobile suite **1,565 tests** and the web suite **489 tests**, with build, type, analysis and formatting checks, and the database suites (PostgreSQL, migrations with 0033, deployment smoke and live orders) passed on disposable clusters. GitHub Actions is not running: every job is refused because the GitHub account is locked for a billing issue, so these checks were run locally before each push to `main`. The release-signed public build **2026092620**, from mobile source `b5323ba`, passed manifest, certificate, all-ABI, public-font/audio and credential-pattern checks.
- **Secrets:** the committed-history scan found no leaks in this audit’s commits (`2cb1551..bedf5b4`). That scan does not certify private/untracked files or later changes.

## Before broader real-money use

| Gap | Required follow-through |
| --- | --- |
| **First real sends and recovery** | Make one small real send (for example 1 USDC to another wallet you own) from the phone and one from the web, and sign in once at `app.trimmy.xyz/wallet-recovery` to confirm the wallet matches. No key was exported and nothing was sent during this audit. |
| **Order fills in production** | Apply migration `0033` with the release command (`npm run db:migrate` with owner credentials), after a fresh backup. The API already writes fills once it exists. |
| **Statements and external activity** | Sends are not yet listed in history. Add sent transfers and network fees before presenting a complete account statement. |
| **Real orders per issuer** | Buy and sell $2 once for each issuer (xStocks, Backpack, Ondo during its session, PreStocks, Tessera) and check the fills in history. Every quote passed the price check read-only on September 28. |
| **Operations** | Set `TRIMMY_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`; add an uptime monitor on `/ready`; fix the GitHub billing lock so CI runs; rotate credentials handled during development; switch the Privy app to production mode; add a `JUPITER_API_KEY` so price and quote reads are not rate-limited. |
| **Policies and issuer eligibility** | Per-issuer eligibility statements, drawn from each issuer's own terms on September 27, are enforced by the server and recorded with each order. They are self-attestations, not identity or residency checks. Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025 treats tokenized instruments as securities; get Nigerian counsel before broad access. Complete counsel-reviewed terms/privacy, operator and contact details and retention requirements. A verified mint, successful simulation or user checkbox is not eligibility approval. |
| **Ondo sessions** | All Ondo tokens are admitted automatically; most quote only in their US sessions, and each says when its market opens. Off-hours spreads can be too wide for the price check, so some orders wait for regular hours. |
| **Card funding** | Crossmint is staging; its test funds cannot finance a mainnet trade. Obtain production onramp approval and validate supported regions, fees, checkout return and actual delivery. Add durable provider-order/webhook reconciliation for support beyond the current status window. |
| **Native reminder validation** | Verify delivery and tap routing on Seeker and iPhone, including denial/revocation, quiet mode, sign-out/account switch, app restart, Android reboot and timezone changes. Android delivery can be delayed by OS battery scheduling. Compile or channel tests alone do not prove delivery. |

Live trading covers the tokens the directory admits automatically; a Market listing alone does not imply trade support. Buys are capped at 100 USDC. Current apps have no per-token sell cap; older installed apps keep the sell limit they display. Some provider routes fail strict review; the app then reports that no route passed and a retry may find another route. No guards were relaxed to admit them.

Live sells currently spend the canonical token account. Tokens in other owned accounts still count toward holdings but require a separately reviewed transfer before they become spendable; no automatic consolidation is implemented.

The in-app notice was checked against implemented flows and the [Privy privacy policy](https://www.privy.io/privacy-policy), [Crossmint privacy policy](https://www.crossmint.com/legal/privacy-policy) and [onramp documentation](https://docs.crossmint.com/onramp/overview). Website policies are a separate surface and still need to stay aligned.

## Valuation

Prices are per displayed share, the unit the wallet's `displayAmount` already uses, so a share multiplier is never applied twice: Jupiter's `usdPrice` and `stockData.price` both track the share (NFLXx at a 10x multiplier: $70.51 traded, $69.55 issuer, $71.44 listed share), and its `usdPricePrescaled` is the per-token price the order check uses with the current multiplier. A thin token's traded price is never used alone. Values are display figures in floating point; orders are still quoted, reviewed and simulated before signing.
