# Production readiness

Status checked September 27, 2026. Trimmy has a working game and a guarded Solana trading path. It is not yet a complete production wallet. This audit separates technical checks from completed transactions and release requirements.

## Verified and fixed

- **75 stock identities from four issuers:** the [server registry](../apps/api/src/stock-trading-registry.generated.ts) binds the original 14 xStocks plus 61 tokens admitted on September 27 (8 xStocks, 37 Backpack, 12 Ondo and 4 PreStocks) to Solana mints. [`stock-admission.mjs`](../tool/testing/stock-admission.mjs) proved each one: pinned issuer authorities and the issuer's own list, mint policy, liquidity, a fair-price check against Jupiter's reference and the underlying share price, and unsigned buy and sell reviews with simulation through the production pipeline. Discovery alone cannot enable trading. Entries are never deleted, only suspended.
- **Order verification:** every admitted token passed an unsigned buy and sell review and simulation on September 27 (sells simulated from large public holders): $1 through Jupiter's aggregator, $2 through market makers, whose $1 minimum applies after fees. The original 14 passed the September 25–26 audit. No transaction was signed or broadcast. The older demo separately records a completed $2 AAPLx buy. Completed sells and completed buys of other stocks remain unverified; liquidity and provider routes can change.
- **Issuer terms and an honest Market:** preview requires the current eligibility statement for the token's issuer (409 `TERMS_REQUIRED`) and stores the acceptance with the order. New builds read capabilities v2: each issuer's disclosure, warning and offer status, and a reason for every Market token that cannot be traded. Installed builds keep the original xStocks-only capabilities and checkbox.
- **All five issuers offered, each with its own warning first:** PreStocks and Tessera are offered at the owner's direction; their disclosures state that PreStocks names no legal issuer, that Anthropic and OpenAI say such share transfers are void, that PreStocks charges a 3% fee on every transfer, and that a Tessera claim is lost if its redemption window is missed. Tessera's three tokens and three PreStocks tokens are refused for now because their pool prices sit far from the reference price.
- **Market-maker (RFQ) orders:** Ondo's liquidity exists only with JupiterZ market makers. The review accepts exactly one market-maker co-signer as fee payer, decodes the order engine's `fill` instruction, requires the maker's own accounts and the exact quoted amounts, and simulates; settlement finds the maker-signed transaction through the user's signature. Ondo's makers mint just in time, after the user signs, so a buy's simulation stops at the maker's transfer. The review accepts that one failure only when the logs show the user's transfer completed and only the maker's delivery failed for insufficient funds, with nothing after it; the fill is all or nothing, and the reviewed terms say so. Twelve Ondo tokens that trade 24/7 passed real buy and sell reviews in Ondo's weekend off-hours window on September 27, the first end-to-end proof of this path. Installed older apps never receive an RFQ order.
- **Honest refusals:** of the 432 Market tokens not tradeable, 330 are Ondo tokens waiting for their market to open, 67 thin xStocks have no market-maker quote, 18 have no confirmed issuer listing (one Ondo token keeps a freeze authority), 11 were too thin or too wide at the weekend check, and 6 trade too far from the reference price. No guard was relaxed to admit a token.
- **Market states:** capabilities v2 gives every token its state now: around the clock, or Ondo's own sessions per token (overnight, pre-market, regular, after-hours, and weekends for some). Ondo's live status page decides, with the published session calendar (pauses, NYSE holidays, early closes, daylight saving) as the fallback, and any mint paused on chain reads as paused by the issuer. Closed tokens cannot be ordered and say when they open; Ondo tokens refused only because their market was closed say when it opens, and read as awaiting review while it is open. A market-maker refusal of an order under the $1 minimum is reported as that, not as a closed market.
- **Fee and share guards:** a buy is refused if a token's transfer fee rises above its admitted rate. Orders in a fee-bearing token widen the slippage tolerance by that fee, because Jupiter quotes before it; the review checks the simulated net delivery. Reviewed terms carry the stock's scaled display multiplier so the app shows shares, not raw units. The review's account read now allows up to 4 MiB: routes through one market maker carry a ~720 KB venue account and were refused every time before. Routes through a third token may use the user's own account for it, and routes may close the user's existing wrapped-SOL account back to SOL; every other token account of the user's in a transaction must end with exactly its starting balance in simulation.
- **Decoder fix with user impact:** SPL Token leaves the old bytes behind an empty option tag after a revoke, which is valid state. The strict account decoder rejected such accounts, so any user whose USDC account ever had a delegate revoked would have had every order refused. Those unused bytes are now cleared before the strict comparison; any other malformed layout still fails.
- **Balances and recovery:** holdings V2 aggregates validated stock accounts and distinguishes total holdings from canonical-account spendable balances. Token-2022 display scaling is preserved. The mobile flow uses trading capabilities, refreshes wallet state, and reconciles pending orders before another submission. Older holdings clients retain V1 responses.
- **Immediate sell readiness:** there is no holding cooldown. A pre-trade request could supply stale balances after confirmation, a zero sell amount could remain frozen, and transaction review checked finalized balances while the app showed confirmed holdings. The fixes force a new post-mutation read at the confirmed slot, reactively populate untouched sell amounts, refresh real routes on return, and use confirmed wallet balances and simulation with a minimum slot. Mint and program identities remain finalized. Tests cover a newly funded account absent from the older finalized snapshot, frozen accounts, paused mints and stale RPC responses. Missing or malformed settlement outcomes stay unresolved; simulation requires explicit success.
- **Server deployment:** `5904ad0` (branch `feat/multi-issuer-trading`) is live in deployment `f58c5cf7-fdf4-4167-bd5d-090d4f9698b8` since September 27, 04:19 UTC, with readiness 200, database healthy and trading enabled. Version 1 capabilities list 21 xStocks in the original shape; version 2 lists 50 tradeable tokens, all five issuers with their offer status and 457 Market tokens with refusal reasons. Unauthenticated previews still return 401.
- **Seeker device build:** build **2026092701** (APK SHA-256 `9ca24837…`, development certificate `95ba33d5…`) was built from a clean build directory, checked for current compiled Dart, and installed with `adb install -r` on September 27; app data was preserved. A read-only walkthrough showed the Real-mode Market with Tradeable markers, SpaceX's five versions with reasons for the three not offered, and the Backpack issuer card with its warning. The eligibility box was not ticked and no quote or trade was requested. The earlier build **2026092619** had shipped stale compiled Dart from September 25.
- **Submitted-order history:** authenticated, paginated Trimmy orders now have pending/confirmed/failed/expired states, explorer links and automatic pending reconciliation. Account changes clear prior rows; pagination and return-from-stock refresh have regression coverage. Amounts are explicitly reviewed quotes, not final fills. Migration `0032` and the history API are deployed. Production readiness, unauthenticated rejection (401), and allowed-origin history/status preflights (204) passed; authenticated history still needs an on-device walkthrough.
- **Gameplay and feedback:** Career/community browser preflights, stale draft revisions, lost draft-save responses and failed sound preparation now have regression coverage. Paper and Real ledgers remain separate.
- **Reminders:** native Android/iOS local scheduling and Career tap routing are implemented for daily or Monday/Wednesday/Friday check-ins around 7 PM local time. This is not a social or transaction push-delivery system.
- **Checks:** on September 27 the backend suite passed **796 tests**, the full mobile suite **1,546 tests** and the web suite **357 tests**, with build, type, analysis and formatting checks. The release-signed public build **2026092620**, from mobile source `b5323ba`, passed manifest, certificate, all-ABI, public-font/audio and credential-pattern checks.
- **Secrets:** the committed-history scan found no leaks in this audit’s commits (`2cb1551..bedf5b4`). That scan does not certify private/untracked files or later changes.

## Before broader real-money use

| Gap | Required follow-through |
| --- | --- |
| **Withdrawals and wallet recovery/export** | Browser recovery is implemented, reviewed and deployed separately, but hidden in the native build. Its current Privy client is native-only (`allowed_domains: ["disallowed"]`). Create a separate web client in the same Privy app with the exact recovery origin, configure the web service, verify sign-in and wallet matching, then enable the native link. No key was exported during this audit. A native send/withdrawal flow is still missing. |
| **Statements and external activity** | Verify deployed submitted-order history on device. Add final executed amounts, fees and external transfers before presenting a complete account statement; reviewed quotes are not fills. |
| **Portfolio valuation** | Add timestamped stock/SOL USD prices and explicit stale/unavailable states, respecting each mint's display scaling. Today the cash card represents USDC, SOL is shown separately, and real stock values are unavailable. Do not present this as total account equity. |
| **Policies and issuer eligibility** | Per-issuer eligibility statements, drawn from each issuer's own terms on September 27, are enforced by the server and recorded with each order. They are self-attestations, not identity or residency checks. Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025 treats tokenized instruments as securities; get Nigerian counsel before broad access. Complete counsel-reviewed terms/privacy, operator and contact details and retention requirements. A verified mint, successful simulation or user checkbox is not eligibility approval. |
| **Ondo tokens outside the weekend window** | Twelve 24/7 Ondo tokens are live. The other Ondo tokens quote only in their US sessions; each is admitted when a real order passes review while its market is open. Off-hours spreads can be too wide for the round-trip limit, so some wait for regular hours. |
| **Card funding** | Crossmint is staging; its test funds cannot finance a mainnet trade. Obtain production onramp approval and validate supported regions, fees, checkout return and actual delivery. Add durable provider-order/webhook reconciliation for support beyond the current status window. |
| **Native reminder validation** | Verify delivery and tap routing on Seeker and iPhone, including denial/revocation, quiet mode, sign-out/account switch, app restart, Android reboot and timezone changes. Android delivery can be delayed by OS battery scheduling. Compile or channel tests alone do not prove delivery. |

Live trading is restricted to the admitted registry; discovery does not imply trade support. Orders are capped at 100 USDC per buy. Each sell is capped at the larger of one unscaled token or about 250 USD worth at admission (the original 14 keep one token). The app shows a specific limit message. Some provider routes fail strict review; the app then reports that no route passed and a retry may find another route. No guards were relaxed to admit them.

Live sells currently spend the canonical token account. Tokens in other owned accounts still count toward holdings but require a separately reviewed transfer before they become spendable; no automatic consolidation is implemented.

The in-app notice was checked against implemented flows and the [Privy privacy policy](https://www.privy.io/privacy-policy), [Crossmint privacy policy](https://www.crossmint.com/legal/privacy-policy) and [onramp documentation](https://docs.crossmint.com/onramp/overview). Website policies are a separate surface and still need to stay aligned.

## Valuation contract still needed

The current Tokens adapter does not establish whether a variant price is per unscaled token or scaled display unit, and preserves undeclared provider timestamp units. Canonical company prices describe the underlying stock. Neither can safely be multiplied by RPC `displayAmount`: a 10× display multiplier could produce a 10× valuation error. [Solana recommends](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount/integration-guide) calculating with unscaled amounts and matching unscaled prices, then converting at the display boundary.

[Jupiter Price V3](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/price) documents mint-keyed `usdPrice`, `decimals` and `blockId`, but the reviewed documentation does not establish its Token-2022 scaling basis. Unauthenticated Price/Tokens reads returned 403; a few authenticated samples would not prove that contract. Existing exact-amount sell quotes can establish indicative **USDC proceeds**, not a USD market price. Their shared 2.1-second request gate and 10-second freshness window make background per-holding quotes unsuitable for portfolio polling.

A feasible provider option is [Birdeye's documented price API](https://bds-support.birdeye.so/hc/en-us/articles/48443824197785-Birdeye-Now-Supports-Scaled-UI-Amounts-for-Solana-Token-2022), which explicitly supports `ui_amount_mode=raw|scaled|both` and supplies `updateUnixTime`. The September 26 read-only check found no configured Birdeye price credential, and its raw-mode endpoint returned 401. The configured Tokens response identified Birdeye as its source but did not establish its raw/scaled unit basis. The issuer’s public AAPLx and NFLXx price endpoints returned null quotes; their schema supplies no quote timestamp. Access, unit basis and freshness therefore remain unverified. Normalize a bounded, cached server price feed to `{network, mint, decimals, priceUsdMicros, unitBasis: "unscaled_token", providerAsOf, observedAt, expiresAt, status}`. Compute `amountRaw × priceUsdMicros / 10^decimals` with fixed-point arithmetic, without multiplying `displayAmount` again. Include SOL and USDC market prices before calling an aggregate USD equity; missing, stale or ambiguous prices must keep the total unavailable.
